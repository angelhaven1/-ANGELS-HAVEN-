WHEN TO COME

For the clearest mountain views, look for an early morning. Clouds often build after midday. Friday and Saturday nights are lively with local hikers from Pokhara; weekdays tend to be quieter.

03 / BEFORE YOU SET OUT

A few things
you may wonder.

Ask us on WhatsApp ↗
Is Australian Camp suitable for beginners and families?＋

Yes. The ridge is a popular first Himalayan outing for weekenders, families and short-trek travelers from Pokhara. It offers high mountain views without a multi-day trek. Walk at your own pace and check current trail conditions before setting out.

Will I get altitude sickness here?＋

Australian Camp sits around 2,060–2,100 m. Many visitors find it a comfortable short stay, but altitude affects everyone differently. Take it easy, stay hydrated, and seek local medical advice if you feel unwell.

Which mountains can I see?＋

On clear days, the panorama includes Annapurna South, Hiunchuli, Machhapuchhre (Fishtail), Dhaulagiri and Lamjung Himal. Visibility changes with weather, and mornings are often clearest.

When is the best time to visit?＋

Arrive early for the best chance of clear skies before clouds roll in after midday. Friday and Saturday nights are often busy with hikers from Pokhara; weekdays are typically quieter.

Can I book a room or meal in advance?＋

Send us a WhatsApp message with your date, group size and what you need. We’ll confirm current availability and details directly.

What facilities and connectivity are available?＋

Enjoy our restaurant, dining hall, outdoor lawn and garden seating. Wi-Fi is available, Ncell and NTC networks reach the area, and hot water or extra blankets can be requested.
